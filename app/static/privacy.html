POLICY

Privacy notice

Effective 14 August 2026

Information CivicLens handles

When you submit a report, CivicLens stores its title, category, description, exact coordinates, uploaded photos, automated image-quality measurements, status, and timestamps. Reports and photos are designed to be publicly visible. The service does not currently provide user accounts.

The hosting provider may also process routine technical logs such as IP addresses, browser details, request paths, and timestamps for security and operation.

How information is used

Information is used to display and manage community issue reports, search by distance, assess photo quality, compare before-and-after evidence, prevent abuse, and operate the service. OpenCV measurements do not identify people or classify hazards.

Service providers

Deployment can use Render for application hosting, MongoDB Atlas for report and photo storage, and Google Maps Platform for the interactive map. Their processing is governed by their own policies, including the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

Do not upload faces, vehicle plates, home interiors, contact details, or other personal or confidential information. If a report needs removal or correction, open a request in the CivicLens GitHub issue tracker and include the report link, but do not post additional private information.

Retention and security

Reports remain available until an administrator deletes them or the demo service is retired. Reasonable technical controls are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. This portfolio deployment should not be used for emergencies or highly sensitive reports.

Changes

This notice may change as the project evolves. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
